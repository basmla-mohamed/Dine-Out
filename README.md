## 🍷 About Dine Out

**Dine Out** is a restaurant website created to showcase a delightful food experience through an attractive interface, elegant colors, and appealing food photography.

The design combines a warm restaurant atmosphere with a modern layout, making the browsing experience visually enjoyable.
